Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

We, at "At-Home Dentures" managed by Dr. Z. Schuber, see utmost importance in providing equitable service to all, including users with disabilities. We have invested significant efforts in making our website accessible to facilitate its use and make it more user-friendly for people with disabilities.

Website Accessibility Information

  • This site complies with the requirements of the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.
  • The accessibility adjustments were made according to the recommendations of the Israeli Standard (IS 5568) for web content accessibility at level AA and the international WCAG 2.1 document.
  • The site is optimized for display on common browsers and for use on mobile phones.
  • The site provides a semantic structure for assistive technologies and supports the standard keyboard usage pattern.

Accessibility Features on the Site

The site includes an accessibility menu that allows for:

  • Increasing and decreasing text size
  • Switching to "high contrast" mode (dark background)
  • Switching to a readable font (Arial)
  • Resetting accessibility settings

Additionally, adjustments have been made for screen readers, and the site has been tested with NVDA software on the Chrome browser.

Physical Accessibility Arrangements

The essence of our service is "At-Home Dentist". All services, from the initial examination and taking impressions to the final delivery of the dentures, are performed in the patient's familiar and comfortable environment (at home, in a nursing home, or any other location).
Therefore, there is no need for physical travel to a clinic, and the service is fully accessible to people with mobility impairments, those who are homebound, and anyone for whom visiting a clinic is a challenge.

Contact for Accessibility Requests and Suggestions

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not be fully accessible. If you encounter an accessibility issue or have a suggestion for improvement, we would be happy to hear from you.
